Golf Games Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email james.e.hable@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

  • Where are my games saved? Only on your phone. They aren't backed up to an account, so deleting the app deletes them.
  • How do friends follow a game? Tap Share in a game and send the code or link. Friends need the app installed; their view is read-only.
  • Live Scoreboard won't start. Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app, then try again.
  • Courses Near Me finds nothing. It lists courses within 3 miles. Search by name instead, or set pars on each hole.
  • Can I find courses outside the U.S.? Not yet. Course search covers U.S. courses only. In the U.K., Ireland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and everywhere else, skip Find a U.S. Course: every hole starts as a par 4, and on each hole screen (in a tournament, the organizer's Rounds tab) you set the par and, with handicaps on, the hole's handicap (stroke index) from the scorecard. Everything else works the same.
  • How are Wolf, 2 vs 2 and 6-6-6 scored? Open the app's menu and tap How to Play: there's a page for each game.
